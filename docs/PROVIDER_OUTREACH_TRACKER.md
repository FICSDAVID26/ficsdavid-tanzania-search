## Mandatory rejection flags

- Requires undisclosed or unaffordable upfront payment.
- Cannot prove authorization for the exact service and Tanzania market.
- Will not provide written prices, commission, refunds or settlement terms.
- Requires FICSDavid to claim a licence or partnership it does not hold.
- Requests API secrets, passwords, customer PINs or OTPs through insecure channels.
