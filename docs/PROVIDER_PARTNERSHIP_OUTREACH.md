Kind regards,
David Mabula

## 3. Questions specific to bundle and airtime partners

1. Are you directly authorized by the mobile operators or an authorized aggregator/reseller?
2. Which Tanzanian networks and exact bundle denominations are available?
3. Is the API able to return a live price and product availability before an order?
4. What is the commission or wholesale discount per product/network?
5. What float is required, how is it funded, and can a small pilot start with no minimum?
6. What are the success, pending, failed and reversal statuses?
7. How are duplicate orders prevented? Do you support idempotency keys?
8. What is the refund/reversal SLA and who bears losses for delayed or failed delivery?
9. Can we test with sandbox numbers without buying float?
10. May FICSDavid market the service under its own brand?

## 4. Questions specific to payment providers

1. What licenses/authorizations does your company hold and what activities may our merchant account perform?
2. Which collections and payouts are supported in Tanzania?
3. What are all fees, taxes, settlement timelines, reserve/hold rules and payout charges?
4. Is sandbox available without a funded production wallet?
5. How are callbacks signed and how can we verify payment status server-to-server?
6. What are the refund, chargeback, reconciliation and dispute procedures?
7. Can you support a platform/marketplace model, or only payments for FICSDavid's own goods/services?
8. What customer data do you process, retain and share?

## 5. Questions specific to SMS/OTP providers

1. Are transactional OTP and marketing messages priced separately?
2. What sender ID registration and TCRA approval steps apply?
3. What are per-network delivery rates, delivery receipts and support escalation times?
4. How are consent, STOP/opt-out and suppression lists enforced?
5. Is a free sandbox or test credit available?
6. What are the API limits, webhook authentication and data retention terms?
